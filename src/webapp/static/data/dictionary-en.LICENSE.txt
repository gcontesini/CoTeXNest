dictionary-en.txt and dictionary-en-GB.txt are the "american-english" and
"british-english" word lists from the Debian "wamerican" and "wbritish"
packages, themselves derived from SCOWL (Spell Checker Oriented
Word Lists), maintained by Kevin Atkinson <kevina@users.sourceforge.net>.
